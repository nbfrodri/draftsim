# DraftSim v0.8.0

## Roster Moves in the Hall

- Browse each reality's market history, including the current unfinished year, newest first by default.
- Filter by year, simulation window, team, multiple regions and roles, movement type, player tier and name. Region, team and role artwork is reused throughout the controls.
- Open player cards and jump from a movement year to its Timeline. Team cards switch between recorded Before/After main and academy rosters, with average tier and graphical team strength.
- Preserve closing-year market news through rollovers. Record actual academy demotions paired with promotions, retirement origin, age and observed inactive tenure when available. Omit retained-player and preseason filter categories.
- Older events without recorded years, origins or roster snapshots remain explicitly unknown; this release does not reconstruct missing historical events or already lost archives.

## Half-star team strength

- Team ratings now span 1 to 5 in increments of 0.5 across season/tournament setup, roster generation, match cards and current/historical profiles.
- Fractions affect normal and bulk simulation, power rankings, strength-based seeding and market attractiveness. Ratings follow teams when sides swap.
- Initial regional targets include half steps while retaining their prior aggregate strength. Individual player tiers and coach ratings keep their own scales.
- Existing integer saves are compatible. Completed results and explicit strengths in already running series are preserved; future roster-derived strengths and simulation probabilities can change.

## Performance and reliability

- Paginate long Records & Dynasties boards in groups of 20, retaining every rank and adding clearer Previous/Next controls.
- Share inactive-reality archive loads across Hall views and avoid redundant writes of histories just read from SQLite. Preserve populated in-memory archives when development hot reload loses loading markers.
- Coordinate restore/read generations and persistence pauses so stale hydration cannot replace restored state. Use SQLite JSON validation for backup checks instead of deserializing large save blobs in Rust.
- Center team logos in live player-search suggestions.

## Compatibility and validation

No physical SQLite schema bump is required for these additions. Optional archive fields remain import-compatible and unknown legacy information is not fabricated. Tests use disposable fixtures, not personal saves.

Before release preparation, 1,374 unit tests and 46 browser tests passed, including half-step selection/save/reload, compiled bulk-worker execution, market cards and filters, multi-year archive persistence, restore guards, Escape navigation and offseason ownership. Types, lint and production build passed. Release preparation also passed all 12 native Rust tests, six version-tool tests and npm audit with zero reported vulnerabilities. The release workflow additionally gates publication on native Rust tests and installed NSIS navigation, reopen and upgrade checks from the pinned 0.6.0 installer. MSI is built and distributed; that smoke test installs NSIS.

Windows x64 installers: `DraftSim_0.8.0_x64-setup.exe` and `DraftSim_0.8.0_x64_en-US.msi`.
