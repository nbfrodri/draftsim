# DraftSim v1.9.0

## Live SS Race

Live Season now shows the current worldwide SS holder for each role, with team logos, player/team cards, average grades, average lane gold differences, win rates and rated-game counts.

Role-filtered contenders show their adjusted SS score, participation requirements, eligibility, gap to the seat and estimated chances. The projection includes completed games in the active phase; actual ownership still changes only at competitive phase reviews. The same selection evaluator serves both the projection and the actual award, including the incumbent's 0.15 replacement margin.

Estimated chances describe uncertainty in recorded results using deterministic resampling. They are not calibrated forecasts of future matches or guarantees of promotion. Players with insufficient rated games have no estimate, and unmet eligibility requirements prevent promotion. Missing grade or gold data stays unknown. The selection rules are available in a keyboard-accessible disclosure, and the panel adapts to desktop and mobile widths.

## Compatibility and installation

Existing realities work without a save migration or a new season. Opening the panel does not assign SS, modify saves or consume the simulator's random state. Existing SS ownership is preserved until the next normal review.

Use the [English installation instructions](../../README.md) or [Spanish installation instructions](../../README.es.md) for Windows and Linux. On Omarchy/Arch, the documented native-build fallback remains available where the AppImage has graphics-library compatibility problems.
