# DraftSim v1.11.0

Follow event performance live, revisit recorded round standings and compare champion picks and bans.

- **Player Rankings** covers each role within a regional split or international event. It shows performance score, average grade, gold difference, games, WR and KDA, with provisional labels for small samples. Players of every underlying tier can lead; rankings assign no tiers or awards.
- Split filters separate regular season and playoffs; international filters separate play-in and main stage when available. Finished games update standings during an unfinished series. Recorded round checkpoints and final event statistics remain available in season history.
- **Most Picked**, **Most Banned** and **Presence** include Top 10/All, role filters and picked-appearance WR. Pick filters use played roles; bans and presence use frozen playable-role metadata. Filters survive collapsing the panel.
- Role leaders include player/team cards and historical team identity. The mobile layout is more compact, and rating, gold and KDA cells explain their recorded sample counts.
- Season SS timeline and annual **Career History → SS history** use historical team logos, split/international icons and clearer status chronology. Live SS Race includes current-team regional league logos. SS selection and gameplay strength rules are unchanged.

Existing realities remain compatible through optional JSON fields; no SQLite schema migration is required. Current games with recorded participants can produce standings, but missing old event archives and unrecorded past checkpoints remain unavailable. Champion boards show current/final stage totals rather than player-checkpoint totals.

Local verification: 1,569 unit tests, 12 native tests, six affected browser tests, TypeScript, ESLint and production export passed. Import/export and repeated SQLite save/load were tested with disposable fixtures. The tagged workflow additionally runs the full browser suite and Windows/Linux installer checks before publishing NSIS, MSI, deb, rpm and AppImage packages.
