# DraftSim v1.10.0

SS ownership is now integrated into season history, player careers and historical cards.

- Season History shows the five SS seats at each recorded competitive-phase review, with player roles, team logos, selection evidence, vacancies and ownership changes.
- Inside **Career History**, each year has an expandable **SS history** block. The career summary distinguishes confirmed acquisitions, review checkpoints held and years with recorded ownership. Renewals and cross-year carry are not new acquisitions.
- Historical event cards distinguish the tier used during competition from SS earned at the closing review. A player can play Worlds as S+ and gain SS after Worlds; both facts remain visible. Career cards identify historical ownership rather than implying a current SS seat.
- New reviews freeze player and review-time club identity. Opening ownership and lifecycle losses preserve continuity through development, benching, transfers and offseason rollover. Recorded reviews are idempotent.
- Team cards explain how SS contributes to roster strength and why half-star rounding or the five-star cap can hide a visible increase. Existing SS selection parameters and gameplay bonuses are unchanged.

Existing realities remain compatible. The new fields are optional additions to the existing browser/SQLite payloads; no database-schema migration is required. Existing SS reviews can be displayed, but missing legacy identities, acquisition dates and intermediate checkpoints remain unknown. Historical selection is not replayed to invent awards.
