# DraftSim v1.15.1

A cleaner Compare timelines view in SS records, SS badges aligned with surrounding text, and lighter mid-year saves.

## SS records

- **Compare timelines redesigned.** One compact column per season, with a tile per player: held seasons show the team logo on the SS colour (hover for the team card and the phases held), a small dot means no SS and a dashed "?" means the season was not recorded. Seasons every selected player held SS are highlighted in gold, with a legend under the grid.
- **Compare from the records.** Each row of "Most SS seasons" has a Compare button that adds or removes the player; it shares the selection with the search box (up to four players).
- Search, role and region filters sit in one toolbar; on phones the role and region filters share a row and the grid scrolls horizontally.

## SS badge

- Inline SS badges after text (for example "Gained SS" in a player's SS history) now sit on the text baseline and are centred on the capitals instead of sitting slightly low.

## Saves

- Events already finished in the season being played are saved without their replay charts (win probability, gold lead and notable events), like archived years. Ratings, KDA and statistics are kept. This makes mid-year saves smaller.

No save or schema change: existing realities and archives open as before.
