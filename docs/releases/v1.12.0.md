# DraftSim v1.12.0

Event Statistics and SS views now follow the DraftSim style, show roles, regions and teams consistently, and open on demand instead of crowding the top of the page.

- **Event Statistics** is collapsible in the season timeline, the season dashboard and live tournaments, closed by default. Stage, checkpoint, role and champion filters survive collapsing and nothing is computed until a panel is first opened.
- Roles read **Top, Jungle, Mid, ADC and Support** with their role icon, regions use their league logo and teams their team logo across Event Statistics, SS history, SS Race and Career History. Role leaders, rankings and champion tables have a refreshed layout that adapts to narrow screens.
- In **Season history**, SS history and Event statistics move below All-Pro Teams under **Season Statistics**. In **Career History**, the SS summary becomes a collapsible **SS career** block after the per-season list; each year keeps its own SS history.
- Legacy seasons without SS reviews read as **Not recorded** and partial careers are labelled instead of showing zeros. SS selection, scoring and gameplay rules are unchanged.

No save or schema change: existing realities and archives open as before, and no SQLite migration is required.

Local verification: 1,574 unit tests, TypeScript, ESLint, the production export and the affected browser tests passed. The tagged workflow additionally runs the full browser suite and Windows/Linux installer checks before publishing NSIS, MSI, deb, rpm and AppImage packages.
