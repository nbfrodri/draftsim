# DraftSim v1.8.0

## Exclusive SS players

- SS is a playable tier above S+, with at most one active holder per position worldwide.
- Holders are reviewed after completed competitive phases. Candidates need an underlying S/S+ tier, enough recorded games, sufficient calendar-relative participation and recent domestic activity. A short hot streak cannot promote a B player directly to SS.
- Selection accounts for individual performance, sample size, consistency and opponent strength. Titles only break exact score ties; incumbents have a replacement margin to limit oscillation.
- SS affects simulation, drafting and market strength. Development and decline continue underneath it; losing the distinction restores the player's evolved underlying tier. Ownership can carry into the next year during a bounded grace period.
- Player cards show the selection evidence and development tier. Saves and archived rosters retain actual SS evidence without fabricating legacy awards.

## Champion meta and presentation

- Live meta evolution uses fresh round evidence rather than repeatedly counting old games. Catch-up processing no longer includes future-round evidence, and patch shifts preserve explicit empty custom overrides.
- MSI's pre-qualified playoff teams display their logos.
- Season Recap's Winningest Team and Most Titles chips open the existing team cards with mouse and keyboard.
- Removed an obsolete market tooltip; simulation-heavy statistical tests retain their full samples with explicit runtime budgets.

## Installation and compatibility

The [English README](../../README.md#install-on-linux) and [Spanish README](../../README.es.md#instalar-en-linux) explain Windows NSIS/MSI, Debian/Ubuntu deb, Fedora/openSUSE rpm and AppImage usage, including the same-machine local-build fallback for Omarchy/Arch.

SS metadata is additive; there is no database schema migration. Existing saves remain readable and completed results are preserved. Champion meta tiers remain D through S+.

The pre-release checks passed 1,537 unit tests, 12 native tests and four focused browser tests. Seeded audits covered 2,592 controlled games plus 2,937 multi-region franchise games, with no duplicate SS holders. Native Linux packaged startup and fresh SQLite initialization were also checked. See the [implementation and verification record](../plans/2026-10-16-season-ss-tier-meta-and-team-cards.md) for sample limits and local packaging diagnostics. Release CI separately validates the tagged revision and creates the Windows and Linux assets before publication.
