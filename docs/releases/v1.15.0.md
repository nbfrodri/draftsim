# DraftSim v1.15.0

Long realities open again on Windows, plus SS records with timeline comparison for any SS holder.

## Opening large saves

- **Saves are read in parts at startup.** A long reality's history could reach hundreds of megabytes and was read in one query, which crossed into the window as a single message and could leave it blank or black on "Restoring your saves". History, saved fragments and the active season are now read in pieces of at most 1 MiB, the same limit already used for writing. There is no save or schema change.
- Very long realities may still take a while to open: years archived before the compact event format are much larger than recent ones.
- Not yet verified on Windows WebView2 with a real large save.

## Records & Dynasties

- **Compare SS timelines for any holder.** Search every player with recorded SS (not only the top 12), filter by role and region, and compare up to four players season by season. Shared SS seasons are highlighted, held cells show the team and split/event icons, and a summary lists SS seasons, longest streak, first and last season, distinct teams and regions. The table scrolls horizontally on phones.
- **Split and event icons** in SS records and in the all-time Event Statistics filters (event and region chips).
- **Hover cards in SS records and all-time Event Statistics** pin each player and team to the most recent season they contributed to within the active filters and show the covered year range.
- Unknown seasons stay "not recorded"; nothing is inferred for legacy years.

No save or schema change: existing realities and archives open as before.
